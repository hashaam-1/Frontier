Inter, by Rasmus Andersson — SIL Open Font License 1.1.
https://rsms.me/inter/

Bundled because the shipped channel style asks for it, and Inter is not
installed on a stock Mac or a stock Windows. Without these files the graphics
fall back to whatever generic sans the machine has, and the layout that was
designed around Inter's metrics stops holding.

They are loaded straight from this folder — nothing needs installing.

EB Garamond (The EB Garamond Project Authors) and Roboto Condensed (The Roboto
Project Authors) — SIL Open Font License 1.1, licence texts in OFL-*.txt.
Bundled for the Documentary look (docgfx.py): EB Garamond for narrative lines,
numbers and quotes, Roboto Condensed for labels, charts and datelines.
